### Multiple sequence Alignment using Clustal Omega
**Aim:**
To perform multiple sequence alignment of homologous proteins obtained from BLAST to find conserved regions.
**Proteins used for alignment:**
1. KRN92659.1 - Query sequence (Lactobacillus amylovours hypothetical protein)
2. WP_014565938.1 - Similar protein from BLAST hit
3. Q831W7 - Similar protein from BLAST hit
**Tool Used:**
EBI Clustal Omega -https://www.ebi.ac.uk/jdispatcher/msa/Clustalo
**Method:**
1. FASTA sequence of above 3 accession numbers were retrieved from NCBI.
2. All sequence were pasted into Clustal Omega input box.
3. Default parameters were used and submitted.
4. Alignment result and phylogenetic tree were downloaded.
**Results:**
- All sequence three protein showed sequence similarity.
- Conserved domains were identified in the alignment.
