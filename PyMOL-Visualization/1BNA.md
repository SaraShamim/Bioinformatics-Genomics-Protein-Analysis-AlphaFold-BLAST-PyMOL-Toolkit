# PyMOL Visualization - DNA (1BNA)
**Project:** B-DNA 3D Structure Visualization
**PDB ID:** 1BNA
### Steps:
Downloaded 1BNA from RCSB PDB
Opened in PyMOL
Used Sticks view for clear nucleotides
Set white background for paper
### PyMOL Commands:
fetch 1BNA
show sticks
bg_color white
ray 1200,1200
### Result:
Publication quality image of DNA structure.
